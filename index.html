Learn

How do I build my first bot?

Open Bot builder, choose a trade type, a stake, and when to buy. Set a stop loss and take profit, then press Run bot. It trades on your demo balance.

What do the digit tools show?

They count the last digit of recent prices. This helps you see patterns in the past, but each new digit is random.

What does the stake multiplier do?

After a loss, the next stake is multiplied. A losing streak grows stakes fast, so keep loss steps low and use a stop loss.

Is real money used here?

No. This demo uses a virtual balance and simulated payouts of 85%. Real accounts would connect through Deriv login.

Is trading risky?

Yes. You can lose some or all of the money you trade. Always practise on a demo account first.
